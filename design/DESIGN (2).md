## Brand & Style

This design system expresses celestial luxury, introspective wisdom, and esoteric sophistication. It is tailored for digital oracles, lunar tarot, and AI-driven symbolic divination. The aesthetic bridges ancient occult iconography with high-end, contemporary digital craftsmanship.

The visual direction draws from **Glassmorphism** combined with **Ambient Luminescence**:
- Deep cosmic atmospheres enriched by ethereal glows, soft planetary halos, and celestial radial backdrops.
- Translucent, frosted glass cards framed by delicate metallic hairpins (antique gold and astral silver).
- Sacred symmetry, delicate astronomical line art, and astral star bursts that orient interaction.
- Tactile, meditative cadence: UI transitions emulate celestial motion, tarot card reveals, and resonant aura pulses without feeling chaotic or carnival-like.

## Colors

The color architecture synthesizes the three ancestral esoteric domains requested: Midnight Eclipse, Astral Plum/Gold, and Arcane Emerald:

- **Primary (`#BFA1ED` - Cosmic Lavender):** The radiant spiritual beacon. Used for primary focal actions, active lunar glow states, shimmering accents, and sacred high-emphasis typography.
- **Secondary (`#C9A46B` - Antique Celestial Gold):** Evokes ancient gilded parchment, metallic foil card rims, star constellations, and ceremonial badges.
- **Tertiary (`#102720` - Arcane Emerald):** Applied selectively to grounded ritual containers, herbal or crystal consultations, and deep verdant aura gradients that interweave with the cosmos.
- **Neutral Base (`#10142C` - Midnight Astral Deep):** The foundational obsidian canvas. Accompanied by `#261027` (Occult Plum) in radial vignettes and under-glows, providing profound atmospheric depth.
- **Surface Elevation Tints:** Translucent vitreous layers built from `rgba(191, 161, 237, 0.08)` to `rgba(201, 164, 107, 0.12)` layered upon dark obsidian backplates.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites poetic mysticism with unyielding legibility:

- **Display & Headings:** Set in `EB Garamond` (evoking classical renaissance grimoires, high editorial literary finesse, and delicate serif serenade). Applied to invocation titles, tarot archetypes, card names, and oracle readings.
- **Interface & Operational Prose:** Set in `DM Sans` for all interactive triggers, buttons, form controls, metadata tags, and lengthy AI divination narratives. Its clean, geometric geometric-grotesque posture balances the ornate headings and guarantees crystal clarity on mobile devices.
- **Casing & Hierarchy:** Eyebrows and sacred tags use `label-sm` in all caps with wide letter tracking (`0.1em`). Headlines maintain natural title casing to sustain a dignified, calm, and reverent demeanor.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an ethereal, symmetrical flow structured through a 12-column adaptive fluid grid (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop):

- **Sanctuary Margin:** Mobile screens enforce a minimum `margin` of `1rem`, expanding to `2rem` on tablets and `3.5rem` on wide desktop viewports to give sacred illustrations breathing room.
- **Vertical Breathing Cadence:** Oracle card spreads require intentional emptiness (`space-xl`) between ritual steps to maintain emotional presence and avoid cognitive clutter during readings.
- **Content Max-Widths:** Oracle reading spreads and reading consultation containers remain centered with a maximum width of `720px` to foster intimacy and focus.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through astral illumination and frosted glass stratification, rather than traditional drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Abyssal Void):** Deep base canvas combining `#10142C` with subtle radial gradients of `#261027` (plum) at the zenith and `#102720` (emerald) at the base.
- **Level 1 (Ethereal Glass Plates):** Secondary cards and background trays utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, a translucent fill of `rgba(25, 20, 52, 0.65)`, and a delicate 1px hairline border of `rgba(191, 161, 237, 0.2)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Ritual Artifacts & Cards):** Tarot cards and interactive options lift with a celestial inner glow (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 16px rgba(191, 161, 237, 0.15)`) coupled with an external diffused aura (`0 8px 32px rgba(16, 10, 36, 0.8), 0 0 20px rgba(191, 161, 237, 0.12)`).
- **Level 3 (Focused Lunar Oracle & Modals):** Full aura blooms using outer dual-tint glows: `0 0 40px rgba(191, 161, 237, 0.35)` combined with antique gold rims `0 0 1px rgba(201, 164, 107, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-shaped and organic arc geometry (`roundedness: 3`).

- **Oracle Buttons & Primary Action Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to evoke celestial completeness, smooth talismanic touchstones, and celestial orbits.
- **Interactive Reading Cards & Portals:** Contained within soft, generous silhouettes using `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) resembling hand-polished oracle card decks.
- **Form Controls & Modals:** Standardized with `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) to keep boundaries supple, warm, and inviting without feeling industrial.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Celestial Glow (e.g., "Obtener mi lectura gratis"):** Full pill-shape filled with a radiant lunar gradient from lavender `#BFA1ED` to soft celestial violet `#9E77DC`. Text set in deep midnight `#10142C` (`label-lg`, `DM Sans`). Features a breathing perimeter aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(191, 161, 237, 0.45)`). On hover, aura increases in luminescence with subtle scale (`1.02x`).
- **Secondary Ritual Touchstones (e.g., "Lectura en vivo", "Lectura virtual"):** Translucent obsidian glass button with rounded rect geometry (`1rem`). Hairline border in antique gold (`#C9A46B`) at 40% opacity or cosmic silver. Contains an icon centered above the label with delicate serifs in `EB Garamond`. On active/hover, border brightens to 100% with gold dust glow.

### Interactive Tarot & Oracle Cards
- Vertical ratio approximating sacred golden proportion (1:1.618).
- Border treated with a 1px dual gradient border (`#C9A46B` fading into `#BFA1ED`).
- Reverberant hover effect: cards gently tilt in 3D parallax space with an under-card aura bloom.
- Card back displays sacred geometry (crescent moon, starlight compass, zodiacal rings). Card front displays gilded frame for the divination reading.

### Input Fields & Ritual Form Controls
- Dark translucent capsule inputs (`background: rgba(16, 20, 44, 0.7)`).
- Hairline stroke in `rgba(191, 161, 237, 0.25)` transitioning to pure `#BFA1ED` upon focus with an ethereal inner vignette.
- Placeholder text in muted astral silver (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`).

### Chips & Constellation Filters
- Pill-shaped tags (`rounded-full`, height: `32px`).
- Inactive: Frosted dark surface with `0.5px` border in muted lavender.
- Active: Gilded outline (`#C9A46B`) with subtle gold particle background tint and bright star icon prefix.

### Symbolic Dividers & Lunar Separators
- Thin hairline divider flanked by starlight glyphs (`✦ ─── ☽ ─── ✦`) rendered in Antique Gold `#C9A46B` at 60% opacity to cleanly demarcate narrative chapters and reading phases.
